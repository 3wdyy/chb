# Tips for Best Results

1. **Personalize every message** — reference the brand's category, recent campaigns, or store count
2. **Time your outreach** — align with seasonal events (Ramadan, DSF, National Days, Back to School)
3. **WhatsApp best practices** — keep messages under 200 words, use voice notes for follow-ups in Arabic
4. **Email best practices** — include a clear CTA, keep subject lines under 50 characters
5. **Arabic dialect matters** — KSA uses "أبي/أبغى", UAE uses "أبا", Kuwait/Bahrain use "أبي" with slight variations
6. **Follow-up cadence** — WhatsApp: 3-5 days after initial; Email: 5-7 days after initial
